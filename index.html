Frequently Asked Questions

For Households / App Users

Q1: How do I start using RecycLink?

A: Download the app, create an account, and list your recyclable items. The system will match you with verified buyers nearby.

Q2: What kind of materials can I sell?

A: Plastics, paper, metals, glass, used electronics, and more. The app automatically categorizes them for you.

Q3: How do I get paid?

A: You can receive payment through GCash, Maya, or bank transfer—or earn EcoPoints redeemable for vouchers and rewards.

For Recyclers / Junk Shops

Q4: How can I register as a recycler or buyer?

A: Go to "Join as Recycler" on the website or app, submit your business details, and get verified by our team.

Q5: How does pricing work?

A: Buyers set their own rates, visible to all users. A recommended "market average" guide helps ensure fair trade.

Trust & Safety

Q6: Are the recyclers verified? How do you prevent scams?

A: Yes. We require valid IDs or business licenses for verification. Verified profiles display Trust Badges for assurance.

Q7: How is my personal data protected?

A: We strictly comply with data privacy laws and use data anonymization to safeguard all user information.

Solutions and Support

Q8: What should I do if I encounter a problem?

A: Contact our support team directly through the app or email support@recyclink.app for quick assistance.

Q9: How are pickup issues and delays managed?

A: Route tracking, notifications, and verified logistics partners ensure reliable scheduling and quick issue resolution.
