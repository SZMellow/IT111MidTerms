RecycLink

RecycLink was formed to fill a growing gap in the recycling industry. The community struggled to sell their recyclables and the recyclers faced limited available material supplies. The platform serves as a digital hub that links users to verified recyclers who offer fair value, convenience, and also trust. RecycLink combines technology and environmental advocacy to make recycling a more fun, transparent, and satisfying process for all involved.

Mission

To provide every household and business to participate in turning waste into value and building a cleaner and more sustainable planet, one transaction at a time.

Vision

To create and ensure a future where recycling is simple, transparent, and rewarding for everyone that bridges the gap between communities and recyclers toward a cleaner and greener world.

Core Values

  • Sustainability – We advocate for environmental care through responsible recycling.
  • Transparency – We ensure fair pricing and honest transactions across our site.
  • Innovation – We use technology to simplify and improve the recycling process.
  • Community – We connect households, junk shops, and recyclers to work together for a shared cause.
  • Integrity – We operate with trust, safety, and accountability in every interaction.
